Quad Lock Introduces Phone Mount With Full Adjustability

Tilt, rotate, and lock your phone as much as you need in one move.

Published on 09.04.2026
Save

Quad Lock has expanded its motorcycle lineup with the launch of the Handlebar Mount Pro Adjustable, adding full 360° adjustability and up to 80° of tilt to its flagship mount while maintaining the secure dual‑stage lock the brand is known for.

The mount features a two‑axis adjustable head that both tilts and rotates, allowing riders to dial in their preferred viewing angle whether they’re running high‑rise bars, raked‑out forks, or simply aiming for a cleaner cockpit. Quad Lock says the design accommodates any fork rake and handlebar rise, extending the versatility of the Pro mount across more motorcycles and riding positions.

A key engineering difference in the new adjustable version is how the angle is set. Most adjustable mounts rely on two‑bolt toothed hinges, with one bolt per axis. That means two separate points that can slip if either isn’t tightened enough. Quad Lock’s new system uses a single bolt to control both tilt and rotation, so riders can set their angle in one motion and lock it down securely. The preload tension system is designed to make fine‑tuning effortless while keeping the mount fixed in place mile after mile.

Senior Product Designer Melvin Choo says the goal was to make angle adjustment faster and more intuitive:

“Our single-bolt head is about getting riders on the road faster, without fiddling with their mount. Adding preload tension takes that a step further, so dialling in your angle feels effortless… Find your perfect viewing angle, lock it in, and ride — it stays there mile after mile.”

The mount is built from anodised CNC‑machined aluminium with black stainless steel hardware and retains Quad Lock’s patented dual‑stage lock for quick, secure phone attachment and removal. Its reversible design also allows riders to position their phone lower and more in line with the handlebars for a cleaner cockpit. It’s compatible with Quad Lock’s motorcycle accessories, including the Vibration Dampener, Anti‑Theft Screw Kit, and charging accessories.

For riders who already own the Handlebar Mount Pro, Quad Lock is also offering the new adjustable functionality as a standalone upgrade. The Adjustable Arm Pro is available separately for $29.99 USD, giving existing users a way to add adjustability without replacing their full mount.

The Handlebar Mount Pro Adjustable launches globally on September 3rd 2026 and retails for $89.99 USD through quadlockcase.com
