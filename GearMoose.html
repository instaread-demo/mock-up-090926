Nike 24.7 Shoes

Recovery is just as important as training, yet it’s often the part athletes overlook. The Nike 24.7 is built around that idea, offering the kind of comfort you appreciate after a workout, long shift, or simply a day spent on your feet.

Rather than creating another running shoe, Nike designed the 24.7 as a dedicated recovery shoe that doesn’t look out of place away from the gym. A stacked foam midsole delivers soft, springy cushioning, while a seamless brushed knit interior wraps your foot in 360 degrees of comfort. The result is a shoe that feels supportive without being overly plush or unstable.

A slip-on design makes them easy to throw on before or after training, while the leather and synthetic upper gives the shoe a more refined appearance than most recovery footwear. Durable foam pods underfoot absorb impact and provide a little extra bounce with every step, and high sidewalls help keep your foot comfortably locked in.

Whether you’re recovering after leg day or simply looking for an everyday shoe that prioritizes comfort, the Nike 24.7 proves you don’t have to sacrifice style to give your feet a well-earned break.

Specs

  • Recovery-focused everyday shoe
  • Stacked foam midsole with responsive cushioning
  • Seamless brushed knit lining
  • Slip-on design
  • Leather and synthetic upper
  • Durable foam outsole with impact-absorbing pods
  • High sidewalls for added stability
  • Men’s and women’s versions available
  • Price: $140
