WoT: “Dragon King” 2D Style [Onslaught 26/27]

A customizable 2D style with a six-stage progression system. When customizing it, you can choose any camouflage stage (awarded for ranks) and equip any emblem earned during the event.

Dragon King —
An all-season style whose elements can be unlocked and obtained in Onslaught mode.

Additional Description

Same as the previous one.

Customizable style — some preset style elements can be changed.

The style customization options include: camouflage (6), emblem (15), and inscription (7).
Camouflages can be obtained by progressing through the ranks, while decals are earned by completing various missions in the mode.

Non-historical. Suitable for vehicles of any nation and tier.
Group: Special.
Technical price: 1,200 gold.
